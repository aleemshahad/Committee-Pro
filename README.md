## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 👥 **Multi-Tier Role-Based Access Control**
Manage committees with complete administrative control:

| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|------------------|
| **Super Head Admin** | Approve committees, manage all users, block/unblock, activate Pro plans |
| **Group Head Admin** | Create committees, track payments, execute lucky draws, manage members |
| **Member** | View payment status, track draw history, receive payment reminders |

### 2. 📊 **Month-by-Month Financial Matrix**
- **Interactive grid** showing all members vs. all committee months
- **Color-coded badges**: 🟢 Paid | 🔴 Pending
- **Quick payment logging** with multiple methods:
  - 💵 Cash
  - 🏦 Bank Transfer
  - 📱 JazzCash / EasyPaisa
- **Receipt verification** and transaction tracking

### 3. 🎡 **Digital Lucky Draw System**
- **Fair & Transparent Draws** with spinning wheel
- **Smart Algorithm** prevents repeat winners
- **Complete Audit Trail** of all draws and payouts
- **One-Click Draw Execution** during monthly settlements

### 4. 🤖 **AI-Powered Payment Reminders**
- **Intelligent Reminder Generator** using Google Gemini
- **Bilingual Support**: English & Urdu
- **WhatsApp-Ready Templates** for payment follow-ups
- **Offline Mode** with pre-built reminder templates

### 5. 🌐 **Bilingual Interface**
- **One-Click Language Toggle** between English & Urdu
- **Authentic Financial Terms**: 
  - قسط (Installment)
  - قرعہ اندازی (Draw)
  - کمیٹی (Committee)
  - بچت (Savings)
- **Culturally Localized** for South Asian communities

### 6. 💰 **Flexible Pricing Model**
- **Free Tier**: Manage up to 2 committees with ads
- **Pro Plan**: Unlimited committees, no ads, automation, exports

### 7. 📈 **Advanced Analytics & Reports**
- Monthly payment summaries with trends
- PDF & Excel export functionality
- Draw history and payout tracking
- Member contribution analysis

## 🚀 Getting Started

### **Quick Start (Web Application)**

1. **Visit the Website**
   - Open your browser and go to the CommitteePro web application

2. **Create Your Account**
   - Sign up with your email and password
   - Choose your role: Admin or Member

3. **Create or Join a Committee**
   - Admins: Create a new committee with members and monthly amounts
   - Members: Join an existing committee
   - Set up payment methods and schedule

4. **Start Managing Payments**
   - Log monthly payments with receipts
   - Track payment status in real-time
   - Generate payment reminders
   - Execute monthly lucky draws

## 💳 Pro Plan Activation

### **For Members: Upgrade to Pro**
1. Tap **"Upgrade to Pro"** button in settings
2. View admin's payment methods (Raast, EasyPaisa, JazzCash)
3. Transfer the amount as instructed
4. Enter **Transaction ID** from receipt
5. Attach **payment screenshot**
6. Submit for admin approval

### **For Admins: Verify Pro Upgrades**
1. Go to **Admin Panel**
2. Navigate to **Pro Requests**
3. Review submitted screenshots and transaction IDs
4. Click **"Accept & Promote to PRO"** or **"Reject"**

### **Pro Features Include:**
✅ Unlimited committees (Free plan: 2 max)  
✅ Ad-free experience  
✅ Automated WhatsApp reminders  
✅ PDF & Excel report exports  
✅ Advanced payment analytics  
✅ Priority support
